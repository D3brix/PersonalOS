# Sourdough basics

## Starter
- Feed 1:1:1 starter, flour and water by weight.
- Ready when it doubles within 4-8 hours at room temperature.

## Loaf (one loaf)
- 500 g flour, 350 g water, 100 g active starter, 10 g salt.
- Bulk ferment until about 50% larger, shape, then retard in the fridge overnight.
- Bake at 250 C in a covered pot for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 20-25 minutes.

## Opinion
A longer cold retard tastes better to me; this is preference, not fact.
