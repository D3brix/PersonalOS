# Crop rotation

Rotating plant families between beds reduces soil-borne disease and balances nutrient use.

## Rotation groups (four-year cycle)
1. **Legumes** (peas, beans): fix nitrogen.
2. **Brassicas** (cabbage, kale, broccoli): heavy feeders; follow legumes.
3. **Roots** (carrots, beets, parsnips).
4. **Solanaceae and others** (tomatoes, potatoes, squash).

## Rule of thumb
Do not grow the same family in the same bed two years running.
